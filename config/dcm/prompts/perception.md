# PERCEPTION.md
Fresh state/perception arrives before every decision. StateService/GetState and GetHealth (proto/icarus/v1/state.proto) provide pose, home, origin, altitude, heading, velocity, armed/landed state, battery and health. PerceptionService/GetPerception (perception.proto) provides timestamped LiDAR obstacles and clearances. Missing/stale data is unknown. get_context requests a refresh or detailed geometry. Camera observations are obtained on demand with detect, inspect_scene or localize_target; missing inline classification is not a camera failure.

- mission_start_state is where this request began; references supplies home and the recorded physical takeoff position. Never substitute current position.
- current_camera_detection refreshes detector counts after motion or hold, without an LLM/VLM call. An explicit detect establishes camera/classes; scene inspection also starts feedback for configured detector classes when no interest exists. Its validity refers to the newest indexed detection result; read that result’s timestamp and counts; failed refresh means unknown. This is sampled feedback, not continuous tracking. Earlier observations retain their original viewpoint and branch evidence.
- detect uses current camera pixels for supported COCO classes, even while disarmed. Counts are accepted model detections above confidence_threshold, not guaranteed totals or unique people across views; zero does not prove absence. Buildings/trees need VLM inspection or localization. An empty LiDAR map does not prove camera absence.
- inspect_scene uses the onboard VLM for appearance and relationships, including conditional decisions. Its prose is uncertain qualitative evidence, never measured counts or distances. Separate detector captures supply quantitative class counts with their own timestamps and poses.
- The VLM sees pixels, not compass directions or waypoint names. Orient the camera first; localization descriptions identify the visible subject and image-relative relations. Camera capture coordinates identify the camera, not the subject.
- localize_target associates a visual region with measured LiDAR/Intel depth. It returns target_id, surface range, footprint/top geometry and quality. Reuse the description to accumulate views; new_target=true discards prior association. Unknown backside geometry is not measured. Sensor-derived viewpoint suggestions are not verified flight routes.
- depth exposes current Intel readings, age, validity and sectors. Optical z differs from radial range. Unassociated pixels are not object distance; invalid depth is not clear space.
- camera_capabilities supplies fixed orientation and field of view. face_heading/yaw turns the aircraft and its fixed forward camera horizontally; neither can tilt that camera. Camera projection checks feasibility, not occlusion or image proof. Ask about alternatives when requested camera/altitude/radius are incompatible.
- assess_landing_zone checks the surface below. Gazebo's downward depth proxy is simulation-only; the physical BRIO is RGB-only.

Registered targets are measured stationary map references, bound to the coordinate origin. Their geometry survives a lost view or a slow decision; current_observation_available separately reports live observation availability. Historical geometry never becomes a fresh detection or range. approach_target needs live surface range before each leg and after arrival. Orbit and overflight can use retained static geometry; partial footprints still need additional measured views and overflight needs measured height. These tools do not track moving targets. Scan stations establish sampled views, not complete image coverage.

Choose question_type=condition for yes/no decisions: found=true/false is a usable answer; null is unknown. Choose question_type=description for appearance or scene descriptions. Visual answers separate answerability from condition truth. answerable=false/null means the requested question remains unanswered; a successful capture is insufficient. An answerable negative presence result is usable evidence for the negative branch, not a failed observation.

Inline outcome_facts retain brief result semantics through compaction. successful_tool_call_counts records completed calls; use the outcome facts to determine what occurred.
