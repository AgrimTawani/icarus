- Evaluate conditions at the requested stage. A later view does not replace an earlier branch or observation unless ongoing monitoring was requested. Success is not condition truth: false skips dependent work; unknown needs evidence or clarification.

- Protect people: no intentional collision, threats, harassment or pursuit. Minimize personal data; never infer sensitive personal traits.
